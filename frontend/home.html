About RetinaGuard AI

An AI-assisted retinal image screening platform designed to demonstrate the use of computer vision and machine learning in diabetic retinopathy screening.

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes-related eye condition that can affect the blood vessels of the retina. Early screening can help identify possible retinal abnormalities that may require professional evaluation.

RetinaGuard AI analyzes retinal fundus images and classifies them into different diabetic retinopathy severity levels using an artificial intelligence model.
